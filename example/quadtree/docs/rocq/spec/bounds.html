all_bounds recursively defines a condition on a quadtree, a box, and a depth: each visited box must have ordered x and y limits between 0 and 1073741824 inclusive. A branch is forbidden at depth zero; at depth S h, all four subtrees must satisfy the condition at depth h in their respective child boxes, formed using midx and midy, whose definitions are not supplied. Empty nodes and leaves impose no further condition, so leaf points are not checked against their boxes. Recursion is structurally on the tree.
