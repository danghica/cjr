overlap

cjr.quadtree.proof.routing.overlap

AI-generated explanation

The definition overlap says that two rectangles b and r overlap when each rectangle’s minimum x-coordinate is strictly below the other’s maximum x-coordinate, and the same holds for their y-coordinates. For rectangles with positive width and height, this means their interiors overlap; merely touching at an edge or corner does not qualify. The supplied rectangle record stores four integer coordinates but imposes no ordering on them, so the definition itself does not require valid, nondegenerate rectangles.

definition overlap:
    parameter(b, r, type(rectangle))
    body:
        and:
            less_than(xmin(b), xmax(r))
            and:
                less_than(xmin(r), xmax(b))
                and(less_than(ymin(b), ymax(r)), less_than(ymin(r), ymax(b)))

Depends on

Original declaration (proof-free)
